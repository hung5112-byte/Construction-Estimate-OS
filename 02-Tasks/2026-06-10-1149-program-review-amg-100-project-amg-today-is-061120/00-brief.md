## Department Head directive — budget re-cost (added 06/15/2026)

**Reframe the objective: hold the schedule, minimize *committed* cash.** The earlier review concluded "spend to protect speed." The Department Head now requires the inverse optimization — **keep the same speed (DVT build target 06/22, cert critical path, 08/18 hard deadline all unchanged) while reducing committed budget.** Quality gates remain inviolable (PRT coverage, two-qualified-sources rule, PCI/EMV rigor, battery validation) — none of the cost moves below may weaken a gate.

Re-cost every de-risk / expedite item in the plan against these rules:

1. **Committed vs. trigger-based.** For each expedite (DG courier, air-freight backup, CM build-team standby hold, expedited pre-stage labor), state whether it must be *committed now* or can be made *conditional on a written trigger* (e.g., "authorize air freight only if supplier ETA slips past 06/17"). Default to trigger-based unless committing now is the only way to hold 06/22. Report committed cash vs. expected cash (committed × trigger probability).
2. **Recoverable working capital ≠ NRE burn.** Treat the Tet / BrightPath pre-build inventory as recoverable capital, not contingency spend. Right-size it to actual ramp demand and move it off the $111.6k program contingency to a separate capital line. Confirm this removes the $15k contingency-reallocation problem.
3. **$0 actions first.** Where a free action (RF-engineer documentation file, contractor search) provides the same schedule protection as a paid one, use it and remove the paid item.
4. **No base-workstream cuts.** The base plan is forecast at $1,978k of $2,000k — do not seek savings by trimming engineering workstreams; savings come only from the new de-risk bundle.

**Deliverable:** the same prioritized improvement plan, but with each action tagged **committed-now** or **trigger-based (trigger + cost-if-fired)**, a committed-cash total and an expected-cash total, explicit confirmation that 06/22 and the 08/18 cert path are unaffected, and explicit confirmation that no action weakens a quality gate.
