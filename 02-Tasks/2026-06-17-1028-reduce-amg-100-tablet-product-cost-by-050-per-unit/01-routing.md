# Task classification

- **Class:** COMPLEX
- **Departments:** 01-hardware-engineering, 02-npi-program-management, 03-quality-reliability, 04-mfg-supplier-quality, 06-finance
- **Reasoning:** This is a cost-down initiative with a hard constraint on no performance regression, requiring trade-off analysis across multiple disciplines. Hardware engineering must evaluate BOM changes (e.g., alternate components) and performance impact. NPI program management must coordinate the change process, ECOs, and supplier schedules. Quality/reliability must define validation gates to ensure no regression in speed or reliability. Mfg/supplier quality must assess supplier readiness and manufacturing yield impact. Finance (06-finance) is needed to track the $0.50 unit cost reduction against the bill of materials and validate the savings. Service operations (05) is not directly needed as the constraint is on product performance, not service deployment. Five departments match the COMPLEX classification for a multi-department debate.
