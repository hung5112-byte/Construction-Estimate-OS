# Glossary (auto-grown)

> The system adds terms automatically when it encounters new ones. The Department Head can edit by hand.

## Hardware & NPI
- **NPI** — New Product Introduction: the process from design freeze to mass production
- **EVT/DVT/PVT** — Engineering/Design/Production Validation Test: the staged build gates
- **BOM** — Bill of Materials: every part in the product, with sources and cost
- **ECO** — Engineering Change Order: the controlled record for any design change

## Supply chain & operations
- **ODM/CM** — Original Design Manufacturer / Contract Manufacturer: the overseas factory partner
- **MOQ** — Minimum Order Quantity a supplier will accept
- **Landed cost** — unit cost + freight + duty + fees: the true cost of an imported unit
- **RMA** — Return Merchandise Authorization: the controlled returns/repair process

## Quality
- **QMS** — Quality Management System (ISO 9001-style)
- **AQL** — Acceptance Quality Limit: the sampling standard for incoming inspection
- **SCAR** — Supplier Corrective Action Request
- **8D** — eight-disciplines structured root-cause method
