### PROMPT 16: Partner / Reseller Agreement Template

#### Description
A partner/reseller agreement template — terms of the relationship, sales commitments, commission mechanics, confidentiality, non-compete, and termination. Should align with the Partner Policy (file 15) and protect both parties. General information only — not legal advice; have a licensed Texas attorney review.

> US legal note: governing law should be Texas. In Texas, a **non-compete** is enforceable only if it is ancillary to an otherwise enforceable agreement and reasonable in time, geographic area, and scope (Tex. Bus. & Com. Code §15.50) [verify with an attorney]. Commission and payment terms are contractual.

#### Information to collect (ask the user before generating)
1. Agreement type? (agent / distributor / referral / affiliate)
2. Term? (1 year / 2 years / open-ended)
3. Minimum sales commitment?
4. Commission payment: frequency? (monthly / quarterly) Conditions?
5. Restrictive covenants? (non-compete / non-solicitation)
6. Confidentiality?
7. Early-termination conditions?

#### Suggested template
Structure:
- **Preamble**: recitals, parties (Party A – Company, Party B – Partner)
- **Section 1** — Scope: products, territory, target customers
- **Section 2** — Term & renewal
- **Section 3** — Sales commitment & KPIs
- **Section 4** — Pricing & discount policy
- **Section 5** — Commission & payment: structure, frequency, conditions, method
- **Section 6** — Party A's rights & obligations
- **Section 7** — Party B's rights & obligations
- **Section 8** — Marketing & brand: co-branding rules, approval
- **Section 9** — Confidentiality
- **Section 10** — Non-compete & non-solicitation (Texas reasonableness standard)
- **Section 11** — Termination: conditions, notice, wind-down obligations
- **Section 12** — Dispute resolution & governing law (Texas)
- **Section 13** — General provisions
- **Exhibits**: product list, commission table, territory map, KPI targets

Confirm the structure before generating.

#### File-generation prompt
```
Create a Partner / Reseller Agreement Template.

CONTEXT:
- Company: [Name] — EIN: [number] — Representative: [name + title]
- Agreement type: [agent / distributor / referral / affiliate]
- Term: [years] — Renewal: [automatic / by agreement]
- Min sales: [amount/month or /quarter]
- Commission: [% + structure] — Payment: [monthly / quarterly]
- Non-compete: [Yes/No] — Scope: [time + territory] (Texas reasonableness)
- Termination: [X days' notice]
- Authority: Texas contract law; non-compete (Tex. Bus. & Com. Code §15.50); governing law: Texas

FORMAT:
- Clear sections, continuously numbered
- Party info: table Party A | Party B — full legal details (EIN, address, representative)
- Commission: table Product | Sales from-to | Commission % | Bonus
- KPI targets: table KPI | Q1 | Q2 | Q3 | Q4 | Annual
- Payment: table Period | Deadline | Condition | Method
- Non-compete scope: territory + duration + product categories
- Termination checklist: 10 items to complete on termination
- Placeholders: [___] for variable info

TONE: Legal — tight but partner-friendly.
LENGTH: 6-10 pages (agreement + exhibits).

NOTE: General information, not legal advice. Have a licensed Texas attorney review (especially the non-compete).

CROSS-REFERENCE: This focuses on the commercial partner relationship. For the standard legal template, see bb-governance/Sample Agency Agreement.
```
