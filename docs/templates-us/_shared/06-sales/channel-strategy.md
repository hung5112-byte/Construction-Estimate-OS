### PROMPT 03: Channel Strategy

#### Description
A comprehensive channel strategy — defining direct/indirect, online/offline, agents, and partnerships, and how to coordinate them omnichannel. The goal: maximize coverage and conversion while optimizing distribution cost.

#### Information to collect (ask the user before generating)
1. Current channels? % of revenue per channel?
2. Which channel performs best? Which is untapped?
3. An agent/distributor network? How many? Where?
4. Online channels? (website, marketplace, social commerce)
5. Any channel conflict? (e.g. agents vs. direct sales)
6. Plans to add channels in the next 12 months?

#### Suggested template
Structure:
- **Part 1** — Channel portfolio: a map of all current + potential channels
- **Part 2** — Direct channels: sales team, website, showroom, inside sales
- **Part 3** — Indirect channels: agents, distributors, resellers, affiliates
- **Part 4** — Digital channels: e-commerce, marketplace, social commerce, app
- **Part 5** — Omnichannel integration: cross-channel customer journey, data sync
- **Part 6** — Channel economics: revenue, cost, margin per channel
- **Part 7** — Channel conflict management: rules, territory, pricing consistency
- **Part 8** — Expansion plan: new channels, timeline, investment, expected ROI
- **Appendix**: channel-partner criteria, territory map, channel P&L template

Confirm the structure before generating.

#### File-generation prompt
```
Create a Channel Strategy.

CONTEXT:
- Company: [Name] — Industry: [industry] — # current channels: [number]
- Current channels: [list + % revenue each]
- Agents: [Yes/No] — Count: [number] — Regions: [list]
- Online: [website / marketplace / social / app]
- Channel conflict: [Yes/No] — [describe]
- Expansion plan: [new channels + timeline]

FORMAT:
- Channel map: overview diagram Company → Channel → Customer (Mermaid)
- Channel scorecard: table Channel | Revenue | % Total | CAC | Margin | Growth | Priority
- Customer journey per channel: Awareness → Consider → Buy → Retain per channel
- Channel economics: table Channel | Revenue | Direct Cost | Margin | ROI
- Conflict-resolution matrix: Situation | Rule | Priority
- Expansion roadmap: 12-month timeline × new channels × milestones
- Resource allocation: table Channel | Headcount | Budget | % total investment

TONE: Strategic, practical, optimization-oriented.
LENGTH: 6-10 pages.
```
