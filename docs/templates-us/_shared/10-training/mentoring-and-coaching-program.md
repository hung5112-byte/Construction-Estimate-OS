### PROMPT 08: Mentoring & Coaching Program

#### Description
A process to set up and run an internal mentoring & coaching program — mentor-mentee matching, session structure, development goals, progress tracking, and program evaluation. This is the "20%" in the 70-20-10 model, highly effective for leadership development and knowledge transfer.

#### Information to collect (ask the user before generating)
1. Main goal: leadership development / onboarding / knowledge transfer / retention?
2. Who are the mentors? (senior leader / manager / subject-matter expert / external)
3. Mentoring style: formal (structured) or informal (organic)?
4. Program duration? (3 / 6 / 12 months)
5. Mentor-mentee meeting frequency? (weekly / biweekly / monthly)
6. Distinguish mentoring (long-term, development) vs. coaching (short-term, performance)?

#### Suggested template
Structure:
- **Purpose & scope**
- **Mentoring vs. Coaching**: distinction, when to use which
- **Eligibility & Selection**: mentor criteria, mentee criteria, nomination process
- **Matching Process**: matching criteria, preference survey, approval
- **Program Structure**: kickoff → monthly sessions → midpoint review → final review
- **Session Guide**: agenda template, conversation starters, goal tracking
- **Mentor Training**: skills mentors need, training for mentors
- **Evaluation**: evaluate mentor, mentee, and overall program effectiveness
- **Appendix**: mentoring agreement, session log, goal-setting worksheet, feedback forms

Confirm the structure before generating.

#### File-generation prompt
```
Create a Mentoring & Coaching Program.

CONTEXT:
- Company: [Name] — Goal: [leadership / onboarding / knowledge transfer / retention]
- Mentor pool: [senior leaders / managers / SMEs / external coaches]
- Style: [formal structured / informal organic / hybrid]
- Duration: [X months] — Frequency: [X times/month]
- Mentoring vs. Coaching: [distinguish / combine]

FORMAT:
- Mentoring vs. Coaching comparison: table Aspect | Mentoring | Coaching — Focus, Duration, Style, Outcome
- Program timeline: Mermaid — Recruitment → Matching → Kickoff → Sessions → Midpoint → Final → Alumni
- Eligibility criteria:
  - Mentor: Min experience | Leadership level | Skills | Commitment | Training completed
  - Mentee: Nomination/Application | Goals | Availability | Manager approval
- Matching process: flowchart — Survey → Preferences → Algorithm/Manual → Propose → Accept → Kickoff
- Mentoring agreement template: Goals | Meeting schedule | Confidentiality | Duration | Expectations | Signatures
- Session structure guide:
  - Session 1: Goal setting — GROW model (Goal, Reality, Options, Will)
  - Session 2-N: Check-in | Progress review | Deep-dive topic | Action items | Next session date
  - Final session: Reflection | Achievements | Ongoing development | Transition
- Conversation-starter library: 20+ questions by theme (career, skills, challenges, network, leadership)
- Mentor training outline: active listening | questioning techniques | feedback models | boundaries | cultural sensitivity
- Tracking template: Session # | Date | Topics discussed | Action items | Status | Mentee progress rating
- Evaluation framework:
  - Mentee assessment: goal achievement % | satisfaction | career impact
  - Mentor assessment: mentee rating | self-reflection | time investment
  - Program KPIs: participation rate | completion rate | goal achievement % | mentee promotion rate | retention rate

TONE: Developmental, supportive, structured yet flexible.
LENGTH: 6-10 pages.
```
