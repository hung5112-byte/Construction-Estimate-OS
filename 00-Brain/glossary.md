# Glossary (auto-grown)

> The system adds terms automatically when it encounters new ones. The Chief Estimator can edit by hand.

## Estimating
- **Takeoff (QTO)**: measuring and counting quantities from drawings and specifications before pricing
- **Class 2 estimate**: AACE 56R-08 bid/tender estimate from construction documents, expected accuracy −5/−10% to +5/+15%
- **Basis of Estimate (BOE)**: the document that records scope, method, data sources, assumptions and exclusions behind an estimate (AACE 34R-05)
- **General conditions (GCs)**: the contractor's project-specific overhead — staff, temporary facilities, safety, cleanup — typically 8–15% of direct cost
- **OH&P**: home-office overhead and profit markup applied after direct cost and general conditions
- **Contingency**: money carried for known-unknowns; set by estimate class and the risk register, not by habit
- **Allowance**: an owner-specified amount for scope whose price is not yet known (Division 01 21 00)
- **Alternate**: an add or deduct price the owner may accept after comparing base bids (01 23 00)
- **Unit price**: a pre-agreed price per unit for quantities that may change (01 22 00)
- **Bid leveling**: normalizing subcontractor quotes to the same scope so the lowest responsible bid is real

## Documents
- **ITB**: Invitation to Bid — the owner's or CM's package that starts a pursuit
- **Addendum**: a change to the bid documents issued before bid day; every addendum must be acknowledged on the bid form
- **Project Manual**: the specifications book, organized by CSI MasterFormat divisions
- **MasterFormat**: CSI's 50-division numbering of specifications and estimate work breakdown
- **UniFormat**: ASTM E1557 systems-based breakdown (foundations, superstructure, enclosure...) used for conceptual estimates
- **Sheet register**: the log of every drawing sheet with number, title, scale, revision and discipline
- **Pre-bid RFI**: a written question to the architect during bidding; the answer arrives as an addendum

## Drawings
- **Title block**: the sheet's identity — number, title, scale, date, revisions
- **Revision cloud / delta**: the marked change and its number on a revised sheet
- **Keynote**: a numbered note on a plan that refers to a legend on the same sheet
- **Schedule**: a table (door, window, finish, equipment, panel) that governs over the plan when they disagree
- **NIC**: Not In Contract — scope shown but excluded from the bid

## Units
- **SF / SY / LF / EA / CY / SQ / TON / LB / SFCA / BCY**: square feet, square yards, linear feet, each, cubic yards, roofing squares (100 SF), tons, pounds, square feet of contact area (formwork), bank cubic yards (earthwork in place)
