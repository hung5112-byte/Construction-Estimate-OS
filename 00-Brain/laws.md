# Laws, regulations and codes relevant to bidding commercial work in Texas

> General information only — not legal advice. Confirm with a licensed Texas attorney, CPA and the surety before relying on any item.

## Contracting and payment (Texas)
- Texas retainage — private work reserved fund 10% for 30 days after completion (Tex. Prop. Code § 53.101)
- Texas public retainage caps — 10% under $5M, 5% at $5M and above, none under $400k (Tex. Gov't Code § 2252.032)
- Texas public work bonds — performance bond over $100k, payment bond over $25k (Tex. Gov't Code ch. 2253)
- Texas mechanic's lien and notice deadlines (Tex. Prop. Code ch. 53)
- Texas Prompt Payment to Contractors (Tex. Prop. Code ch. 28)
- Texas sales and use tax on construction — lump-sum vs separated contracts, new construction vs nonresidential remodel (Texas Comptroller Pub. 94-116; Tex. Tax Code ch. 151)
- Texas prevailing wage on public work (Tex. Gov't Code ch. 2258)

## Federal
- Davis-Bacon prevailing wages on federally funded work over $2,000 (40 U.S.C. § 3141)
- OSHA construction standards (29 CFR Part 1926)
- Fair Labor Standards Act (29 U.S.C. § 201)
- Federal bid procedures where applicable (FAR Part 14)

## Contract forms and precedence
- AIA A101 / A201 general conditions; drawings and specifications are complementary (AIA A201 § 1.2.1)
- AIA A701 Instructions to Bidders; AIA A310 bid bond; AIA A305 contractor qualification (AIA A701-2018)
- ConsensusDocs 200 — specifications govern in a direct conflict (ConsensusDocs 200)

## Codes that drive scope and cost (verify the adopted edition per jurisdiction)
- International Building Code as adopted by the city (IBC 2021, local amendments)
- International Energy Conservation Code (IECC 2021) — envelope and lighting power
- NFPA 13 sprinkler design and NFPA 72 fire alarm (NFPA 13 / NFPA 72)
- National Electrical Code (NFPA 70 / NEC 2023)
- Texas Accessibility Standards (TAS 2012) and ADA (28 CFR Part 36)
- Stormwater / SWPPP under the TPDES construction general permit (TXR150000)
