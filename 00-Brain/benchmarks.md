## Sanity ratios
- General conditions: 8–15% of direct cost (SmartBarrel / Procore)
- Division 01: 4.5% low / 8.1% average / 16.2% high of total (DCD/BNi)
- Labor share of direct cost: ~60% (ConstructConnect)
- Structural steel: 5–10 psf for single-story commercial (SteelFlo)
- Sub quote more than 15–20% below the median of leveled bids → red flag (Buildr)
- Engineer's estimate vs low bid: within ±10% (FHWA)
- QSR restaurants: kitchen / ventilation / grease systems 30–40% of cost (Terrapin)
