# Bid/no-bid and estimating procedures

> ⚠️ **SYNTHETIC DEMO DATA** — fictional procedures of Blackland Commercial Builders (BCB), adapted from published GC practice (MeltPlan bid/no-bid template, ContractsConnected bid-day checklist, EC&M estimating department checkup).

## Bid/no-bid (within 48 hours of the ITB, before sub invitations)
Score 1–5 on each, weighted: owner quality & payment history 25% · project fit (building type, size, location) 20% · resource capacity (estimating bandwidth, PM/superintendent availability, bonding, cash flow) 20% · competitive position (number of GCs, incumbents, pre-committed subs) 20% · strategic value 15%.
- ≥ 3.5 pursue · 2.5–3.4 Chief Estimator judgment call · < 2.5 decline

## Estimating sequence (every bid)
1. Log the package: sheet register, spec index, addenda log, bid date, RFI cutoff, site visit date
2. Declare the AACE class and the accuracy band in the project profile
3. Read Division 00/01 in full before any takeoff
4. Takeoff by discipline with sheet-and-revision provenance; schedules govern; figured dimensions over scaling
5. Send sub ITBs with scope sheets; minimum three bidders per package
6. Consolidate questions; issue pre-bid RFIs at least 5 business days before the cutoff
7. Price from `03-Cost-Library/` first; published data second and marked
8. General conditions from the schedule; markups per `markup-policy.md`
9. Second-person review 24 hours before bid day using the bid-day gate below
10. Turnover package to operations within one week of award

## Bid-day gate (reviewer is never the lead estimator)
Every trade has a real sub bid or a documented plug · lowest responsible sub is the one in the summary · sub bid validity dates checked · sub exclusions reconciled (temp power, hoisting, equipment setting, commissioning, final clean, permits) · owner allowances carried verbatim · alternates labeled add/deduct · unit prices consistent with the base bid · arithmetic ties from takeoff to summary · general conditions match the current duration · markups per the authority matrix · addenda acknowledged · bond and insurance rates current · scale gate passed on every measured sheet · open CRITICAL RFIs listed as assumptions with cost exposure.
