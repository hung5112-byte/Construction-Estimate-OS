### PROMPT 06: Franchise Model Blueprint

#### Description
A blueprint for designing the whole franchise model — unit economics, territory design, fee structure, support model, and franchise lifecycle. The "architectural drawing" before building the franchise system. General information only — before franchising in the US you must prepare an FTC-compliant FDD; consult a franchise attorney.

> US note: before you sell franchises, federal law (FTC Franchise Rule, 16 CFR Part 436) requires a **Franchise Disclosure Document (FDD)** with 23 specified items, delivered to prospects 14 days before signing. Texas requires a one-time Business Opportunity filing for FTC-compliant franchisors (Tex. Bus. & Com. Code ch. 51) [verify]. Build the model with these obligations in mind.

#### Information to collect (ask the user before generating)
1. Is the current business model standardized? (SOP, training, brand)
2. Unit economics: average revenue/cost/profit per unit?
3. How many company-owned units now? Piloted a franchise yet?
4. Territory: by city / county / state / region / national?
5. Planned fees: initial franchise fee + royalty + marketing fund?
6. Support model: training, marketing, supply chain, IT, ongoing coaching?

#### Suggested template
Structure:
- **Part 1** — Franchise readiness assessment: checklist — is the business ready to franchise?
- **Part 2** — Unit economics: a sample P&L for one franchise unit — revenue, costs, payback, ROI
- **Part 3** — Fee structure: initial fee, ongoing royalty %, marketing fund %, other fees
- **Part 4** — Territory design: map, exclusive zones, non-compete radius
- **Part 5** — Franchisee profile: the ideal candidate — background, capital, commitment
- **Part 6** — Franchise lifecycle: Recruit → Train → Launch → Support → Renew/Exit
- **Part 7** — Support model: pre-opening, ongoing operations, marketing, technology
- **Part 8** — Growth projections: # units Year 1-5, revenue projections, break-even per unit
- **Appendix**: franchise-readiness scorecard, unit P&L template, FDD-preparation checklist (FTC Rule)

Confirm the structure before generating.

- FDD-prep checklist: the 23 FTC items to compile (with a franchise attorney)
