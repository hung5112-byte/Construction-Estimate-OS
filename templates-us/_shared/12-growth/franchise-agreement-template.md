### PROMPT 09: Franchise Agreement Template

#### Description
A franchise-agreement template defining the rights and obligations between Franchisor and Franchisee under US/Texas law. General information only — franchising is heavily regulated; this MUST be prepared and reviewed by a licensed franchise attorney.

> US legal note: in the US, franchising is governed primarily by the **FTC Franchise Rule (16 CFR Part 436)**, which requires the franchisor to give each prospective franchisee a **Franchise Disclosure Document (FDD)** at least **14 calendar days** before any agreement is signed or money is paid. Some states add registration/relationship laws. **Texas** requires a one-time **Business Opportunity exemption filing** with the Secretary of State for franchisors that comply with the FTC Rule (Texas Business Opportunity Act, Tex. Bus. & Com. Code ch. 51) [verify current requirement]. Trademark protection is via the USPTO. [verify all specifics with a franchise attorney]

#### Information to collect (ask the user before generating)
1. Franchise type? (full business format / product / master franchise)
2. Term? (5 years / 10 years / other)
3. Fees: initial fee, royalty, marketing/ad fund — specific amounts?
4. Territory: exclusive or non-exclusive?
5. Renewal rights? Conditions?
6. Has a franchise attorney been engaged? (this template REQUIRES attorney review and a compliant FDD)

#### Suggested template
Structure:
- **Section 1** — Definitions & glossary
- **Section 2** — Grant of franchise: use of brand, system, know-how
- **Section 3** — Territory & exclusivity
- **Section 4** — Term & renewal
- **Section 5** — Fees: initial, royalty, marketing/ad fund, other
- **Section 6** — Franchisor obligations: training, support, supply, marketing
- **Section 7** — Franchisee obligations: compliance, reporting, quality, confidentiality
- **Section 8** — Intellectual property: brand/trademark use (USPTO), IP protection
- **Section 9** — Inspection & monitoring
- **Section 10** — Transfer/assignment
- **Section 11** — Termination: causes, consequences, post-term non-compete (Texas reasonableness)
- **Section 12** — Dispute resolution & governing law (Texas)
- **Section 13** — General provisions
- **Exhibits**: territory map, fee schedule, SOP list, training program, brand guidelines

Confirm the structure before generating.

#### File-generation prompt
```
Create a Franchise Agreement Template.

CONTEXT:
- Company: [Name] — EIN: [number]
- Franchise type: [full / product / master]
- Term: [years] — Renewal: [conditions]
- Fees: Initial [___] | Royalty [___]% | Marketing [___]% (USD)
- Territory: [exclusive / non-exclusive]
- Authority: FTC Franchise Rule (16 CFR Part 436, FDD required 14 days pre-signing); Texas Business Opportunity Act (Tex. Bus. & Com. Code ch. 51); USPTO (trademark); governing law: Texas

FORMAT:
- 13 continuously numbered sections — US legal language
- Fees: summary table — Fee type | Amount/% | Frequency | Payment method (USD)
- Franchisor obligations: 10-15 item checklist — Training | Support | Marketing | Supply | IT | QC
- Franchisee obligations: 15-20 item checklist — Compliance | Reporting | Quality | Branding | Payment
- IP protection: brand-use rules — do's and don'ts (USPTO trademark)
- Termination matrix: Cause | Notice period | Consequences | Non-compete duration (Texas reasonableness)
- Dispute resolution: negotiation → mediation → arbitration (AAA) → Texas courts
- Signature block: both parties, date
- Exhibit list: A (Territory) | B (Fee Schedule) | C (SOP list) | D (Brand Guide) | E (Training Program)

⚠️ LEGAL NOTE: This template MUST be reviewed by a licensed franchise attorney. You must prepare and deliver a compliant FDD (FTC Rule) at least 14 days before signing, and complete any Texas Business Opportunity filing.

TONE: Legal, formal, balanced between the parties.
LENGTH: 10-15 pages.

NOTE: General information, not legal advice.
```
