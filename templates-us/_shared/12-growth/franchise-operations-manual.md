### PROMPT 07: Franchise Operations Manual

#### Description
A comprehensive operations manual for the franchisee — the "bible" covering everything from setup, daily operations, staffing, and local marketing, to financial reporting. A "clone" of the SOP package (departments 01-12) customized for the franchise context. Note: the operations manual is typically referenced in, and disclosed via, the FDD — have a franchise attorney coordinate it.

#### Information to collect (ask the user before generating)
1. Is the SOP package complete? (reference departments 00-12)
2. Must the franchisee comply 100%, or is there room to customize?
3. Brand guidelines: logo, color, signage, uniform — detail?
4. Supply chain: where does the franchisee buy? (central / approved vendors / open)
5. Technology: POS, CRM, reporting — what systems?
6. Compliance: inspections, audits, mystery shopping — frequency?

#### Suggested template
Structure:
- **Chapter 1** — Welcome & brand: history, mission, values, brand promise
- **Chapter 2** — Pre-opening: site selection, build-out, hiring, training, soft opening
- **Chapter 3** — Daily operations: opening/closing procedures, service standards, quality control
- **Chapter 4** — Staffing: hiring, training, scheduling, performance management
- **Chapter 5** — Products/services: menu/catalog, preparation standards, quality specs
- **Chapter 6** — Customer experience: service standards, complaint handling, loyalty programs
- **Chapter 7** — Marketing: local-marketing guidelines, approved materials, social media
- **Chapter 8** — Financial management: bookkeeping, reporting to franchisor, royalty payment
- **Chapter 9** — Technology: POS setup, CRM, reporting dashboard
- **Chapter 10** — Compliance: standards, inspections, corrective actions
- **Chapter 11** — Emergency procedures: crisis management, PR, insurance
- **Appendix**: checklists per chapter, contact directory, FAQs

Confirm the structure before generating.

#### File-generation prompt
```
Create a Franchise Operations Manual (Table of Contents + key chapter outlines).

CONTEXT:
- Company: [Name] — Industry: [industry]
- Customization: [100% compliance / some customization]
- Supply chain: [central / approved vendors / open]
- Technology: [POS / CRM / Reporting tools]
- Compliance: [inspection frequency]

FORMAT:
- Table of Contents: 11 chapters + appendix — numbered sections
- Per chapter: outline 5-10 sections × key content summary
- Chapter 3 (Daily Ops) most detailed: morning checklist ☐ | service flow | closing checklist ☐
- Chapter 8 (Financial): reporting requirements — what | when | how | to whom
- Brand-guidelines section: logo do's/don'ts | color codes | signage specs | uniform
- Compliance scorecard: Category | Standard | Score (1-5) | Weight | Weighted score → Total
- Quick-reference cards: 1-page summaries for daily use per chapter
- Index: alphabetical topic index for quick lookup

TONE: Clear, prescriptive, franchisee-friendly — "how-to" not "what-to".
LENGTH: 10-15 pages (outline + key chapters; full manual will be 50-100+ pages).
```
