### PROMPT 17: Partner Onboarding SOP

#### Description
A standardized process from receiving a new partner → evaluation → signing → training → go-live → review. Ensures the partner is equipped with the knowledge, tools, and support to sell effectively from day one.

#### Information to collect (ask the user before generating)
1. Current partner-evaluation process? How many steps?
2. Who owns onboarding? (Channel Manager / Sales Manager / a dedicated team)
3. What does training cover? (product, process, CRM, marketing)
4. Average onboarding time? (1 week / 2 weeks / 1 month)
5. Partner toolkit: what to prepare? (docs, accounts, materials)
6. New-partner review: after how long? Criteria?
7. A probation period?

#### Suggested template
Structure:
- **Purpose & scope**
- **Process overview**: a 7-step flowchart — Receive → Evaluate → Approve → Sign → Train → Go-live → Review
- **Step 1 — Receive**: receive application, initial screen, respond
- **Step 2 — Evaluate**: due diligence, scoring, interview
- **Step 3 — Approve**: submit for approval, conditions, notify result
- **Step 4 — Sign**: prepare agreement, sign, set up accounts
- **Step 5 — Train**: a 5-10 day program, quiz/test
- **Step 6 — Go-live**: first-deal support, shadowing, check-in
- **Step 7 — 30/60/90 review**: evaluate, feedback, adjust, graduation
- **Onboarding Toolkit**: a checklist of all documents/tools to prepare
- **Appendix**: training curriculum, partner welcome kit, evaluation form

Confirm the structure before generating.

#### File-generation prompt
```
Create a Partner Onboarding SOP.

CONTEXT:
- Company: [Name] — Partner type: [agent / distributor / affiliate]
- Owner: [Channel Manager / Sales Manager / Team]
- Onboarding time: [weeks / month]
- Training content: [product / process / CRM / marketing]
- Toolkit: [list docs + tools]
- Probation: [Yes/No] — [term]
- Review: [30 / 60 / 90 days]

FORMAT:
- Flowchart: Mermaid — 7 steps + overall timeline + responsible
- Per-step detail: table Step | Actions | Owner | Duration | Output | Checklist
- Evaluation scorecard: table Criterion | Weight | Score 1-5 | Pass/Fail threshold
- Training curriculum: table Day | Module | Content | Duration | Trainer | Deliverable
- Onboarding checklist: 30+ items ☐ — grouped by Pre-sign | Post-sign | Pre-go-live | Post-go-live
- 30/60/90 review: table KPI | 30-day target | 60-day target | 90-day target | Actual | Status
- Partner welcome kit: a list of all documents + links + descriptions
- Graduation criteria: criteria table → Pass = official partner | Fail = extend/terminate

TONE: Standard SOP — welcoming but with a clear process.
LENGTH: 6-8 pages.
```
