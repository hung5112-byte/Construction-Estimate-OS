# PROMPT 16: Joint Venture / Cooperation Agreement Template

#### Description
A cooperation (joint-venture) agreement template — a business cooperation that does NOT form a new legal entity. Governs contributions, division of work, profit/loss sharing, and joint management. Governed by Texas contract law. General information only — not legal advice.

#### Information to collect (ask the user before generating)
1. Purpose of the cooperation? (a specific project / shared business / R&D / market entry)
2. What does each party contribute? (cash / assets / technology / people / brand)
3. Profit/loss split?
4. Who manages day-to-day operations?
5. Term? Termination conditions?
6. Any foreign party? (additional rules may apply)

#### Suggested template
Structure:
- **Section 1** — Parties, legal capacity
- **Section 2** — Purpose & scope of cooperation
- **Section 3** — Contributions: a table of what each party contributes, and its value
- **Section 4** — Allocation of work: who is responsible for what
- **Section 5** — Management & decisions: a joint steering committee, voting
- **Section 6** — Finances: budget, separate accounting, profit/loss split
- **Section 7** — Intellectual property: who owns IP created during the cooperation
- **Section 8** — Confidentiality & non-compete
- **Section 9** — Term, renewal, termination, wind-down
- **Section 10** — Force majeure, dispute resolution
- **Appendix**: contribution table, projected budget, KPIs

Confirm the structure before generating.

#### File-generation prompt
```
Create a Joint Venture / Cooperation Agreement template.

CONTEXT:
- Purpose: [description]
- Parties: [Name + cash/asset/resource contribution]
- Profit/loss split: [detail]
- Management: [Party A / Party B / joint steering committee]
- Foreign party: [Yes/No]
- Governing law: Texas contract law (consider forming a separate LLC/JV entity for liability — confirm with attorney)

FORMAT:
- Clearly numbered sections
- Contribution table: party | contribution type | value | % | timing
- Allocation table: area | responsible | accountable | budget
- P&L sharing: a clear formula + a worked example
- Exit mechanism: withdrawal conditions, buy-out of a contribution, wind-down

TONE: Legal, tight, balanced.
LENGTH: 10-15 pages.

NOTE: General information, not legal advice. Have a Texas attorney review before signing.
```
