# P-PPL-08: Leave / PTO Policy

#### Description
A policy detailing the types of leave — paid time off (PTO/vacation), sick leave, parental/family leave, bereavement, unpaid leave, and holidays. General information only — not legal advice; confirm with an employment attorney.

> US legal note (important): US federal law does NOT mandate paid vacation, paid sick leave, or paid parental leave, and Texas has no state PTO or paid-sick-leave mandate. Paid leave is largely at the employer's discretion. The main legal frameworks are:
> - **FMLA** (Family and Medical Leave Act, 29 U.S.C. §2601): up to 12 weeks of UNPAID, job-protected leave — applies only to employers with 50+ employees and to eligible employees [verify eligibility]. A solo operator below that threshold is generally not covered.
> - **PWFA / Pregnancy Discrimination Act** (EEOC): reasonable accommodation for pregnancy/childbirth.
> - **USERRA** (military leave), **jury duty** (Texas requires unpaid time off for jury service — Tex. Civ. Prac. & Rem. Code §122.001), and protected time to **vote** (Texas Election Code §276.004) [verify specifics].
> - Federal holidays (11) are not required to be paid days off for private employers.

#### Information to collect (ask the user before generating)
1. PTO/vacation: how many days? (no legal minimum — common US practice is ~10-15 days; entirely discretionary) Does it accrue with tenure?
2. Sick leave: separate bucket or part of PTO? (no Texas mandate)
3. Parental leave: any paid benefit, or unpaid (FMLA if 50+ employees)?
4. Bereavement / other leave: how many days per case? (discretionary)
5. Unpaid leave: max days? Conditions?
6. Request process: app / email / paper? How far in advance?

#### Suggested template
Structure:
- **Part 1** — PTO/vacation: days, accrual, carryover, payout at separation (note: Texas does not require PTO payout unless your policy/agreement promises it — Texas Payday Law, Tex. Lab. Code ch. 61 [verify])
- **Part 2** — Sick leave: amount, documentation, discretionary nature
- **Part 3** — Parental/family leave: FMLA (if covered) + any company paid benefit; short-term disability if offered
- **Part 4** — Bereavement / jury duty / voting / military leave
- **Part 5** — Unpaid leave: conditions, limits
- **Part 6** — Holidays: which holidays the company observes (private employer's choice)
- **Part 7** — Request process: flowchart + advance-notice timeline
- **Appendix**: leave-type summary table, request form

Confirm the structure before generating.

#### File-generation prompt
```
Create a Leave / PTO Policy.

CONTEXT:
- Company: [Name] — Industry: [industry] — Headcount: [number]
- PTO/vacation: [days] — Accrues with tenure: [Yes/No]
- Sick leave: [separate / part of PTO]
- Parental leave: [paid benefit / unpaid / FMLA if 50+]
- Max unpaid leave: [days]
- Process: [app / email / paper] — Advance notice: [days]
- Authority: FMLA (29 U.S.C. §2601, 50+ employees); EEOC/PWFA (pregnancy); Texas — no state PTO/sick-leave mandate; Texas Payday Law (Tex. Lab. Code ch. 61, payout of earned wages per policy)

FORMAT:
- 1-page summary table: Leave type | Days | Paid? | Documentation | Advance notice | Approver
- PTO accrual calculator: hire month → first-year pro-rated PTO
- Request flowchart: employee submits → manager approves → HR records → balance updated
- Parental/medical leave: FMLA eligibility note + any company benefit + claim process
- Holiday calendar: the holidays the company observes
- FAQ: 10 common questions — "Do I lose unused PTO?", "Is sick leave paid?"

TONE: Clear, fair, with specific legal references and US-accurate caveats.
LENGTH: 5-7 pages.

NOTE: General information, not legal advice. US/Texas mandate little paid leave — most terms here are the employer's choice. Confirm with a licensed Texas employment attorney.
```
