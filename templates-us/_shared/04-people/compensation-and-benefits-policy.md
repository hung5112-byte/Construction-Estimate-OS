# P-PPL-06: Compensation & Benefits Policy

#### Description
A detailed policy on pay structure (base + KPI/variable + bonus), pay bands, the pay-review process, raise policy, performance bonuses, and year-end bonuses. General information only — not legal/tax advice; confirm FLSA exempt/non-exempt classification and payroll-tax treatment with an attorney/CPA.

#### Information to collect (ask the user before generating)
1. Current pay structure? (100% base / 70-30 / 50-50 base-variable?)
2. Do you have clear pay bands yet?
3. Pay-review frequency? (annual / semi-annual)
4. Bonus policy: year-end bonus? KPI bonus? Project bonus? Equity?
5. Probationary/introductory pay = what % of full pay?
6. Any market salary benchmark?

> US legal note: year-end bonuses are discretionary in the US — there is no statutory bonus requirement. Pay must comply with the FLSA minimum wage (federal $7.25/hour — 29 U.S.C. §206; Texas follows the federal minimum [verify current rate]) and overtime rules for non-exempt employees. Texas has no state income tax, but federal income tax, FICA (Social Security 6.2% + Medicare 1.45%), and FUTA/SUTA payroll taxes apply.

#### Suggested template
Structure:
- **Part 1** — Pay principles: fair, competitive, performance-linked
- **Part 2** — Total compensation: base + variable/KPI + allowances + bonus
- **Part 3** — Pay bands: grade/level × min-mid-max
- **Part 4** — Pay-review process: timeline, criteria, approval
- **Part 5** — Bonus policy: KPI bonus, year-end bonus (discretionary), spot bonus
- **Part 6** — Benefits & allowances: health insurance, retirement (e.g. 401(k)), stipends
- **Part 7** — Introductory pay, part-time pay, overtime pay (FLSA)
- **Appendix**: detailed pay bands, benefits/allowance table

Confirm the structure before generating.

#### File-generation prompt
```
Create a Compensation & Benefits Policy.

CONTEXT:
- Company: [Name] — Industry: [industry] — Headcount: [number]
- Pay structure: [e.g. 70% base + 30% variable]
- Pay bands: [Yes/No] — # levels: [number]
- Pay review: [frequency]
- Bonus: [year-end / KPI / project / equity]
- Introductory pay: [% of full pay]
- Authority: FLSA (29 U.S.C. §206-207, minimum wage/overtime); FICA (26 U.S.C. §3101); Equal Pay Act / Title VII (equal pay); Texas — no state income tax

FORMAT:
- Structure visual: pie chart — base | variable | allowances | bonus
- Pay bands: table Grade | Level | Min | Mid | Max | Notes (USD)
- Pay-review process: flowchart — self-assessment → manager review → calibration → owner approval
- Bonus table: Bonus type | Conditions | Formula | Timing | Approval
- Benefits/allowance table: Type | Amount | Eligibility | Notes
- FAQ: 10 common compensation questions
- Note exempt/non-exempt classification matters for overtime [verify with attorney]

TONE: Internal, clear, fair — employees must understand their pay.
LENGTH: 8-12 pages.

NOTE: General information, not legal/tax advice. Confirm FLSA classification and payroll-tax handling with an attorney/CPA.
```
