# P-PPL-05: Workplace Conduct & Work Rules

#### Description
A written set of work rules and workplace-conduct standards: hours, conduct, workplace safety, protection of property and trade secrets, and a discipline framework. In the US there is no government-registered "internal labor regulations" document; instead these rules typically live in an employee handbook. They are not legally mandated, but they are strongly recommended and should be consistent with the at-will relationship. General information only — not legal advice; have an employment attorney review.

> US legal note: the US has no government filing/registration requirement for internal work rules — they live in an (unfiled) employee handbook. These rules support an at-will employment relationship and federal/Texas compliance.

#### Information to collect (ask the user before generating)
1. Work hours? (8h/day? shifts? flexible?)
2. Regular overtime? (note: under the FLSA, non-exempt employees must receive overtime at 1.5× for hours over 40/week — 29 U.S.C. §207 [verify exempt/non-exempt classification with an attorney])
3. Specific conduct/discipline rules to list? (industry-specific)
4. Any special property / trade secrets to protect?
5. Industry-specific safety requirements? (factory / job site / office — OSHA, 29 U.S.C. §651)
6. Required uniform/dress code?

#### Suggested template
Structure:
- **Section I** — General: scope, who it applies to; at-will statement (employment may be ended by either party at any time, with or without cause — Texas default)
- **Section II** — Hours & breaks: schedule, overtime (FLSA non-exempt OT rules), timekeeping
- **Section III** — Workplace conduct: dress code, behavior, use of company property
- **Section IV** — Safety & health: workplace safety rules (OSHA)
- **Section V** — Protection of property, trade secrets, and IP
- **Section VI** — Anti-harassment & anti-discrimination: EEO policy and complaint channel (Title VII / EEOC; Texas Labor Code ch. 21 — Texas extends sexual-harassment provisions to employers with as few as one employee, Tex. Lab. Code §21.141 et seq. [verify current scope])
- **Section VII** — Progressive discipline: forms of discipline, process (apply consistently; at-will preserved)
- **Section VIII** — Employee responsibility for company property
- **Section IX** — Acknowledgment & effective date

Confirm the structure before generating.

#### File-generation prompt
```
Create a Workplace Conduct & Work Rules document.

CONTEXT:
- Company: [Name] — Industry: [industry] — Headcount: [number]
- Hours: [start-end] — Off days: [which days]
- Overtime: [Yes/No] — Frequency: [describe]
- Special safety: [describe]
- Uniform: [Yes/No]
- Authority: FLSA (29 U.S.C. §201, wages/overtime); OSHA (29 U.S.C. §651, safety); Title VII / EEOC + Texas Labor Code ch. 21 (anti-discrimination/harassment); Texas Payday Law (Tex. Lab. Code ch. 61, wage payment); at-will employment (Texas)

FORMAT:
- Sections covering hours, conduct, safety, property/IP, anti-harassment, discipline
- Include a clear at-will statement and a statement that the document is not an employment contract
- Legal but plain language for all employees
- Hours table: Day | Start | End | Meal break
- Discipline table: Violation | Severity | Action | Notes (apply consistently)
- Anti-harassment section: definition + reporting channel + investigation process
- Footer: effective date, acknowledgment signature line

TONE: Clear, serious, US/Texas-compliant.
LENGTH: 12-18 pages.

NOTE: General information, not legal advice. Have a licensed Texas employment attorney review. No government registration of this document is required in the US.
```
