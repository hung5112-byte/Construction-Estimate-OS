### PROMPT 08: Email Marketing SOP

#### Description
A comprehensive email-marketing process — from list building, segmentation, content, and automation sequences, through A/B testing, to analytics and optimization. Includes compliance (CAN-SPAM; GDPR for EU subscribers) and deliverability best practices.

> US compliance note: commercial email must comply with the **CAN-SPAM Act** (15 U.S.C. §7701; 16 CFR Part 316) — accurate "From"/subject lines, a valid physical postal address, and a working unsubscribe honored promptly. SMS marketing is governed by the **TCPA** (47 U.S.C. §227). GDPR applies only if you email EU residents. [verify specifics]

#### Information to collect (ask the user before generating)
1. Email-marketing platform? (Mailchimp, HubSpot, GetResponse, SendGrid?)
2. Current list size? Growth rate?
3. Email types? (Newsletter, Promotion, Onboarding, Abandoned cart, Re-engagement?)
4. Send frequency? (weekly / twice a week / monthly?)
5. Any automation sequences? (Welcome series, drip campaigns?)
6. Current average open rate / click rate?

#### Suggested template
Structure:
- **Step 1** — List building: lead magnets, opt-in forms, segmentation strategy
- **Step 2** — Segmentation: criteria, tags, dynamic segments
- **Step 3** — Content creation: subject line, preview text, body, CTA, design
- **Step 4** — Automation setup: welcome series, nurture sequence, triggers
- **Step 5** — A/B testing: subject lines, send time, content, CTAs
- **Step 6** — Send & monitor: deliverability, bounces, complaints, CAN-SPAM compliance
- **Step 7** — Analytics & optimization: open rate, CTR, conversions, unsubscribes

Confirm the structure before generating.

#### File-generation prompt
```
Create an Email Marketing SOP.

CONTEXT:
- Company: [Name] — Platform: [Mailchimp / HubSpot / GetResponse / SendGrid]
- List size: [number] — Growth rate: [number/month]
- Email types: [Newsletter / Promo / Onboarding / Abandoned / Re-engagement]
- Frequency: [frequency]
- Automation: [Yes/No] — Sequences: [list]
- Current metrics: Open [%] | Click [%]
- Compliance: CAN-SPAM (15 U.S.C. §7701) — address + unsubscribe; TCPA for SMS; GDPR if EU subscribers

FORMAT:
- 7-step flowchart: Mermaid — List Build → Segment → Content → Automate → Test → Send → Analyze
- List-building strategies: 5-7 tactics — Lead magnet | Form placement | Incentive | Expected conversion
- Segmentation matrix: table Segment | Criteria | Size | Content Type | Frequency
- Email template library: 5 templates — Welcome | Newsletter | Promo | Re-engagement | Transactional
- A/B test protocol: table Element | Variant A | Variant B | Sample % | Duration | Winner = ?
- Automation workflows: 3 flows — Welcome (5 emails) | Nurture (7 emails) | Re-engage (3 emails) — timeline + content outline
- CAN-SPAM checklist: accurate header | physical address | working unsubscribe | honor within 10 business days
- Performance benchmarks: table Metric | Industry Avg | Our Target | Action if Below

TONE: Standard SOP, conversion-focused, data-driven.
LENGTH: 6-8 pages.
```
