### PROMPT 09: Social Media Playbook

#### Description
A comprehensive playbook for social media — per-platform strategy, posting schedule, content mix, engagement rules, social crisis management, and guidance for working with influencers.

> US note: sponsored posts and influencer partnerships must clearly disclose the material connection (e.g. #ad, #sponsored) per the FTC Endorsement Guides (FTC Act, 15 U.S.C. §45). Advertising claims must be truthful and substantiated. [verify specifics]

#### Information to collect (ask the user before generating)
1. Main platforms? (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, X?)
2. Social goals? (brand awareness, community, lead gen, customer service?)
3. Current / target posting frequency? Per platform?
4. Current content mix? (% educate / entertain / sell / behind-the-scenes?)
5. Do you work with influencers?
6. Any prior social-media crisis? Handling process?

#### Suggested template
Structure:
- **Platform Strategy**: goal + strategy for EACH platform
- **Content Mix**: ratio — Educate / Entertain / Engage / Sell
- **Posting Schedule**: frequency, best time, calendar template
- **Engagement Protocol**: how to reply to comments, DMs, mentions, reviews
- **Crisis Management**: situation classification, escalation, response templates
- **Influencer Guidelines**: selection criteria, brief template, performance measurement, FTC disclosure requirements
- **Brand Voice on Social**: tone, dos and don'ts, good/bad examples

Confirm the structure before generating.

#### File-generation prompt
```
Create a Social Media Playbook.

CONTEXT:
- Company: [Name] — Platforms: [Facebook / IG / TikTok / YouTube / LinkedIn / X]
- Social goals: [awareness / community / leads / support]
- Posting: [frequency per platform]
- Content mix: Educate [%] | Entertain [%] | Engage [%] | Sell [%]
- Influencers: [Yes/No] — Influencer budget: [amount]
- Crisis history: [Yes/No]
- Compliance: FTC Endorsement Guides (disclose #ad/#sponsored)

FORMAT:
- Platform profile cards: per platform — Goal | Audience | Content type | Frequency | KPIs | Best time
- Content mix pie: 80/20 or custom — Value vs. Sell ratio
- Weekly posting schedule: table Day × Platform — Content type + time slot
- Engagement SOP: table Situation | Response time | Tone | Template | Escalation
- Crisis playbook: Level 1 (negative comment) → Level 2 (bad viral) → Level 3 (crisis) — response flow
- Influencer evaluation: table Criterion | Weight | Scoring — Followers | Engagement | Relevance | Cost | Brand fit + required FTC disclosure
- Do/Don't list: 10 Do's + 10 Don'ts on social — with concrete examples

TONE: Social-savvy, brand-conscious, practical.
LENGTH: 6-8 pages.
```
