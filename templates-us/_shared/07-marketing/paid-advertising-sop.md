### PROMPT 06: Paid Advertising SOP

#### Description
A standardized process for running Facebook/Google Ads — from planning, campaign setup, creative production, launch, and optimization, to reporting. Includes an A/B testing protocol, budget management, and escalation when performance is poor.

> US advertising-law note: ads must be truthful, not misleading, and claims must be substantiated (FTC Act, 15 U.S.C. §45; FTC advertising guidance). Disclose material connections in endorsements/influencer content (FTC Endorsement Guides). Avoid unsupported "#1"/health/earnings claims. [verify specifics with an attorney for regulated claims]

#### Information to collect (ask the user before generating)
1. Main ad platforms? (Facebook/Meta Ads, Google Ads, TikTok Ads, LinkedIn Ads?)
2. Monthly ad budget? Split per platform?
3. Main ad objective? (Traffic, Leads, Conversions, Brand Awareness?)
4. Who runs ads? (In-house / Agency / Hybrid?)
5. A creative-approval process before launch?
6. Reporting frequency? (Daily / Weekly / Monthly?)

#### Suggested template
Structure:
- **Step 1** — Planning: objective, audience, budget, timeline
- **Step 2** — Audience setup: targeting, custom audiences, lookalike
- **Step 3** — Creative production: ad copy, visual, video, CTA (truthful, substantiated)
- **Step 4** — Campaign setup: campaign structure, ad sets, placements
- **Step 5** — Launch & monitor: go-live checklist, daily monitoring
- **Step 6** — Optimize: A/B testing, bid adjustment, audience refinement
- **Step 7** — Report & learn: performance report, insights, next steps

Confirm the structure before generating.

#### File-generation prompt
```
Create a Paid Advertising SOP.

CONTEXT:
- Company: [Name] — Platforms: [Facebook / Google / TikTok / LinkedIn]
- Ad budget: [monthly] — Split: [Facebook %] [Google %] [Other %]
- Objective: [Traffic / Leads / Conversions / Awareness]
- Team: [In-house / Agency / Hybrid]
- Creative approval: [Yes/No] — Approver: [who]
- Reporting: [Daily / Weekly / Monthly]
- Compliance: FTC Act (truthful, substantiated ads; disclose material connections)

FORMAT:
- 7-step flowchart: Mermaid — Planning → Setup → Creative → Launch → Monitor → Optimize → Report
- Campaign brief template: Objective | Audience | Budget | Timeline | KPIs | Creative brief
- A/B testing protocol: table Variable | Version A | Version B | Duration | Sample Size | Winner Criteria
- Daily monitoring checklist: ☐ Spend | ☐ CPC/CPM | ☐ CTR | ☐ Conversions | ☐ ROAS | ☐ Anomalies
- Optimization triggers: table Metric | Threshold | Action — e.g. CTR < 1% → review creative
- Budget pacing: table Day × Planned Spend × Actual × Variance (USD)
- Report template: executive summary | KPIs | top campaigns | insights | recommendations
- Compliance check: claims substantiated, disclosures present

TONE: Standard SOP, performance-focused, data-driven.
LENGTH: 6-8 pages.
```
