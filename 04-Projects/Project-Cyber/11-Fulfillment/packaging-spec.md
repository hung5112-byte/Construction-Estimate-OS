> ⚠️ SYNTHETIC DEMO DATA · Hub: [[Project-Cyber-Hub]] · Owner: Tom Becker · Dept: [[01-Departments/01-hardware-engineering/index|Hardware Eng]]

## Retail/commercial pack (single unit)
| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| Inner box | Recyclable molded-pulp tray (cost-down ECO, no foam) |
| Contents | CY-80, USB-C cable, quick-start card, compliance leaflet |
| Drop spec (packaged) | ISTA 3A pass, 10-drop sequence |
| Master carton | 5 units, edge-crush rated for 4-high pallet |
| Pallet | 200 units / pallet (40 cartons) |

## Accessory packs
- CYS-10 cradle: separate carton, region power adapter SKU
- Battery (service): UN38.3-compliant Li-ion pack, depot/field replenishment
- CY-80R refurb: brown-box service pack, advance-exchange labeled

## Sustainability / cost
- Molded-pulp tray replaced EPS foam at Y1 cost-down (−$1.20/unit, recyclable) → [[cost-walk]]
- Right-sized master carton cut dimensional weight on parcel lanes → [[logistics-plan]]

## Compliance
- UN38.3 + IATA for Li-ion air shipments; battery state-of-charge limited for transport
- Labeling: model, FCC/ISED/CE/UKCA marks, SN/IMEI (CY-80L), country of origin
